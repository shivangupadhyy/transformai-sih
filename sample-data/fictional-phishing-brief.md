# Fictional Incident Brief: SecureDocs Phishing Campaign

**Classification:** Demonstration material — fictional  
**Date:** 2 September 2026

The fictional National Cyber Coordination Centre (NCCC) has identified a targeted phishing campaign impersonating **SecureDocs**, a fictional document-sharing service used by public-sector and critical-infrastructure organisations.

Between 28 August and 1 September 2026, the NCCC received 37 reports of emails claiming that a "shared incident file" required urgent review. The emails directed recipients to lookalike websites designed to capture usernames, passwords, and one-time verification codes. The campaign primarily targets communications, procurement, and IT personnel.

No confirmed compromise of the genuine SecureDocs platform has been identified. However, six organisations reported that employees entered credentials on fraudulent pages before the pages were reported and blocked.

The NCCC recommends that organisations remind staff that SecureDocs notifications originate only from the official `securedocs.example.gov` domain. Recipients should not open links from unexpected messages, should report suspicious emails to their IT security team, and should reset passwords and notify security teams immediately if credentials were entered on a suspicious site.

The NCCC is coordinating with internet service providers to disrupt malicious domains and will issue updates if the campaign changes materially.
